# CMS O&C Weekly Meeting

## Summary

The discussion outlined the operational, development, and communication responsibilities of a new role shared between [[Submission Infrastructure]] and [[Frontier]] operations. The group established that responsive site support and reliable operations take precedence over development objectives.

## Decisions / Conclusions

- The role was described as nominally split between Submission Infrastructure and Frontier operations, with the actual allocation to evolve according to operational need.
- Operations—including site onboarding, tickets, validation, monitoring, and timely communication—are the primary responsibility.
- Development and monitoring improvements are valuable but secondary to maintaining operations.

## Action Items

None explicitly recorded.

## Discussion

### Role scope

Factory-operation work includes communicating with sites, opening and following tickets, onboarding resources, validating entries, and monitoring distributed infrastructure. Frontier work covers monitoring Frontier/Squid infrastructure, cache effectiveness, site misconfigurations, and the load placed on central services at CERN and Fermilab. The group noted a smaller development component for both areas.

### Objectives and priorities

The discussed objectives were: becoming familiar with the infrastructure; becoming able to support new factory-resource integration independently; producing per-pilot CPU-efficiency metrics; supporting heterogeneous-resource entries; and improving monitoring and alerting. The group regarded the operational-readiness objective as the most urgent deliverable. The per-pilot CPU-efficiency work was a concrete development project, but its timing should not displace operational duties.

### Working practice

Participants stressed responding rapidly while a site administrator is actively debugging an entry, since long delays break the feedback loop. When operations are stable, remaining time can be invested in the next development or monitoring task.
